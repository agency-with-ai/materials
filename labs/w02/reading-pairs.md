# Lab 2 reading pairs

## Normalization paper pair

This is the default pair. It assumes both of you can already explain feedforward networks, mini-batches, means and variances, and gradient descent.

Your team is picking a normalization method for a network trained with small mini-batches. Compare Layer Normalization with Batch Normalization.

**Reading question.** How does the choice between the two methods change one example's dependence on the other examples in its batch?

| Role        | Paper                                                                                                                                                                |
| ----------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Focal       | Ba, Kiros, and Hinton, [Layer Normalization, arXiv v1](https://agencyai.mit.edu/lab2/files/papers/normalization/layer-normalization-v1.pdf) ([source](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1607.06450v1))               |
| Predecessor | Ioffe and Szegedy, [Batch Normalization, ICML 2015](https://agencyai.mit.edu/lab2/files/papers/normalization/batch-normalization-icml2015.pdf) ([source](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v37/ioffe15.pdf)) |
